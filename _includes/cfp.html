We invite submissions to the EMNLP {{ page.year }} workshop MINT: Multimodal Interaction in Face-to-Face Dialogue. The workshop brings together researchers in computational linguistics, natural language processing, computer vision, HCI, robotics, and cognitive science working on multimodal face-to-face communication.

We welcome work on computational models that integrate verbal and non-verbal cues such as speech, text, gesture, gaze, facial expression, and body pose; multimodal datasets and resources; evaluation methods; and applications involving embodied conversational agents, social robots, and behavioural analysis.

Submission format

Scope

We welcome submissions on multimodal face-to-face interaction, including but not limited to multimodal dialogue modelling, gesture-speech integration, gaze and turn-taking, multimodal grounding, behavioural annotation, embodied conversational agents, social robotics, and evaluation methods for multimodal communication systems.

Selection criteria

Submissions will be evaluated for relevance to the workshop, technical quality, clarity, novelty, and potential to stimulate interdisciplinary discussion across NLP, computer vision, HCI, robotics, and cognitive science.

Presentation format

Accepted contributions will be invited for presentation at the MINT workshop co-located with EMNLP {{ page.year }} in Budapest, Hungary. Presentation formats may include posters and selected contributed talks, depending on the final program.
